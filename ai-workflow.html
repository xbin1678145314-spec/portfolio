AI-assisted Game Art Workflow

At my current studio I use GPT and image-generation tools in production, and I participated in writing and strengthening the studio's AI-assisted art workflow. The goal is not to replace art decisions, but to shorten exploration and preparation time while keeping the final result artist-controlled and Unity-ready.

What AI is used for

Requirement analysis, visual reference generation, composition exploration, asset-reference preparation and AI-assisted modeling support.

What remains artist-controlled

Visual direction, composition decisions, style consistency, asset cleanup, technical constraints, quality control and final Unity implementation.

Why it matters

The workflow reduces repetitive exploration work and helps move from a text requirement to production-ready references faster.

Production pipeline

Requirement Analysis
        ↓
Visual Reference Generation
        ↓
Composition Exploration
        ↓
Asset Reference Creation
        ↓
AI-assisted Modeling Support
        ↓
Artist Refinement & Quality Control
        ↓
Unity Integration & Validation

How I use the workflow

  • Break down text requirements into visual goals, asset needs and production risks.
  • Generate visual references and composition options before committing to production.
  • Create clearer asset references, including views that help modeling and production.
  • Use AI-assisted modeling only as a starting point when it is efficient, then manually refine the asset.
  • Check topology, style, scale, materials, performance constraints and Unity presentation before delivery.

Production principle

AI accelerates exploration. The artist remains responsible for decisions, consistency, cleanup, technical validity and the final game-ready result.

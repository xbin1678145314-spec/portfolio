Unity Mobile Technical Art & Optimization

My technical-art work is currently focused on art-side mobile optimization: locating rendering problems, understanding why Batches or draw calls increase, adjusting assets and materials, and working with programmers when a code-side implementation is required.

Diagnosis

Unity Stats and Frame Debugger are used to identify repeated draws, material changes, batching breaks and unnecessary rendering work.

Asset-side fixes

Mesh simplification, material consolidation, texture control, scene organization and transparent-material review are applied where appropriate.

Programmer collaboration

When batching, runtime mesh combination, visibility control or other implementation changes require code, I provide the visual/performance requirement and verify the result.

Typical analysis flow

Performance Issue
        ↓
Unity Stats / Frame Debugger
        ↓
Identify Draw / Material / Mesh Cause
        ↓
Art-side or Programmer-side Solution
        ↓
Unity Validation

Case: high-instance scene

A scene reached approximately 300 Batches. After reviewing mesh complexity, materials and batching conditions, I simplified the relevant assets and worked with the programmer on batching changes. The scene was reduced to approximately 150 Batches while preserving the intended visual result.

Other optimization problems I review

  • Different meshes and materials preventing efficient batching.
  • Transparent preview materials causing a large increase in Batches and overdraw.
  • Large construction scenes with many visible objects at the same time.
  • Camera view range increasing the amount of rendered scene content.
  • Texture size, mesh density, material count and unnecessary multi-material setups.
  • Visual QA after implementation to confirm the optimization did not damage presentation.

Current technical direction

I am continuing to deepen my understanding of Unity rendering fundamentals, Shader Graph, URP, CPU/GPU responsibilities, transparency, depth and render queues so that I can move from practical optimization toward a stronger art-focused Technical Artist role.

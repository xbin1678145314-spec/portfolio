OPTIMIZATION

Production-side technical work

I use technical tools to solve art-production problems rather than presenting myself as a deep rendering engineer.

CASE 01

300 → 150 Batches

A scene with many similar low-poly objects produced excessive batches. Mesh simplification plus batching adjustments with the programmer reduced the measured scene Batches from roughly 300 to 150.

Frame DebuggerDynamic BatchingMesh Reduction
CASE 02

Large construction scene diagnosis

On a larger senior-care construction project, I checked split build pieces, transparent previews, same-material low-poly tiles and camera visibility when Batches became difficult to control. The final build state used replacement/merged meshes, while the camera range was also treated as part of the rendering budget.

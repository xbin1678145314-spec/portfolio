Unity optimization

Art-side performance work focused on mobile rendering cost, batching, meshes, materials and scene organization.

Batch reduction

A high-instance scene reached about 300 Batches. After mesh/material review and batching changes with the programmer, it was reduced to about 150 while preserving the intended visual result.

Large construction scene

Performance review covered split construction meshes, transparent preview materials and camera view range to reduce the amount of rendered content on screen.

Rendering diagnosis

I use Unity Stats and Frame Debugger to trace repeated draws, material changes, batching breaks and other art-side rendering costs before proposing fixes.
